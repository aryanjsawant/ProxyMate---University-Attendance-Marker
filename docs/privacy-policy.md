# Privacy Policy — ProxyMate

**Last updated: 11 August 2026**

## The short version

ProxyMate collects nothing, stores nothing off your device, and sends nothing anywhere.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — your subjects, your timetable, your attendance records and your settings — is written to a single file in the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing else has access to it.

That data never leaves the device unless *you* explicitly export it and choose where to send it, using the Export option in the app.

## What the app collects about you

Nothing. Specifically:

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- No name, phone number, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Network access

The released app **does not request the Android internet permission**. It is technically incapable of transmitting your data anywhere, whether to us or to anyone else. You can verify this yourself: the permission list on the Play Store listing, or in Android's app info screen, contains no network access.

## Permissions the app does request

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show the end-of-day reminder and low-attendance warnings. Optional — decline it and the rest of the app works normally. |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | To re-register those reminders after your phone restarts. Without it, notifications would silently stop after a reboot. |
| `VIBRATE` | Standard notification behaviour. |

## Children

ProxyMate is intended for students in higher education and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete, and no account to close. You can also wipe it from inside the app with **More → Reset everything**.
